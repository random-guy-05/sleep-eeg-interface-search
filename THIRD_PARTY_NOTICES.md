# Third-party resources

The pretrained model, underlying Human Sleep Project/PhysioNet data, Python dependencies and IEEE authoring template are separate works with their own terms. This release does not include pretrained model weights, individual-level data or IEEE template assets.

- Model: https://github.com/bdsp-core/philosophers-stone (repository identifies CC BY-NC 4.0 for its software/assets; consult the actual upstream license and checkpoint terms).
- Data and outcome definition: https://moody-challenge.physionet.org/2026/ and applicable Human Sleep Project access/data agreements.
- Published model-development manifests: https://github.com/bdsp-core/philosophers-stone/tree/master/paper/provenance . Linked by source and checksums, not redistributed with patient outcomes.
- IEEE Access template, delivered separately to the authors: https://ieeeaccess.ieee.org/authors/submission-guidelines/ . Downloaded official May 13, 2026 template; original class/fonts retained.
- NumPy, pandas, SciPy, scikit-learn, PyArrow, Matplotlib, PyYAML and other Python packages are installed by the package manager, not vendored as dependency source.

The release retains original research-code provenance and notices. It does not infer a license for previously unlicensed files or claim authorship of upstream model/data/template material.
